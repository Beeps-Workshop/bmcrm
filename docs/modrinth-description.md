**_Beep's Mean Crystalline Resource Machine (BMCRM) is a mod built around resonating crystals. Form blank crystals, infuse them with a resource, then let a Resonator put them to work._**

You can be generating resources right after your first mining trip. Most resource-gen mods start "too late" for my taste (sorry [Mystical Agriculture](https://modrinth.com/mod/mystical-agriculture) ily), so BMCRM keeps the entry tier cheap and simple.

You'll need just these three machines:
- **Crystal Former** – form blank crystals from glass, amethyst, prismarine, echo shards or shulker shells!
- **Crystal Infuser** – infuse a material\* into a blank crystal to make a dedicated resource crystal!
- **Resonator** – put your crystals to work and reap their resource!

_\* material detection is tag-based; not every mod tags things properly, so exceptions might exist._

## Modulators

![Modulators in a row](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/67a65593eaee25c940314c8e664d16614c47124f.gif)

Modulators let you augment nearby crystals. Each one behaves a little differently, so experiment to find the layout that suits you best!

- **Silk Touch** – generates ore blocks instead of the normal drops. Affects a 3×3 area.
- **Fortune** – grants 1 level of Fortune (up to a max of 5) to crystals in a `+` shape (5×5).
- **Auto-Smelt** – automatically smelts the items generated. Affects any crystal that sits between it and the Resonator.

## Dependencies

BMCRM does not depend on any mods directly, but **optionally integrates** with the following:

- [JEI](https://modrinth.com/mod/jei) – Recipes, Machines & Crystals
- [JADE](https://modrinth.com/mod/jade) – Shows crystal information
- [Create's Ponder](https://github.com/Creators-of-Create/Ponder) – All technical blocks have Ponders
- [AlmostUnified](https://modrinth.com/mod/almostunified) – Respects any altered outputs
