# Resonance Ingredients

Crafting ingredients made in the [Crystal Crucible](../Machines/CrystalCrucible.md) by steeping a
vanilla item in Liquid Resonance. Higher [crystal tiers](./CrystalTiers.md) and the
[Modulators](../Machines/Modulators/SilkTouch.md) are crafted from them.

| Ingredient            | Made from           | Liquid Resonance | Used for                                        |
|-----------------------|---------------------|------------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| Activated Lapis Lazuli| Lapis Lazuli        | 25 mB            | Modulator Base, Auto-Smelt Modulator            |
| Shining Crystals      | Prismarine Crystals | 100 mB           | Tier 3 crystals, Silk Touch & Fortune Modulators |
| Pulsing Pearl         | Ender Pearl         | 250 mB           | Tier 4 crystals                                 |
| Resonating Gem        | Diamond             | 1000 mB          | Tier 5 crystals                                 |
